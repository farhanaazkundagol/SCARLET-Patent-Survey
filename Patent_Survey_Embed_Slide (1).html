Patent Survey: SCARA & Vision Pick-and-Place Systems

KLE Tech • Dept. of Automation & Robotics • Team 08 (MSD Project)

SL YR PATENT NO. PATENT TITLE INVENTOR(S) PROBLEM ADDRESSED PROPOSED SOLUTION TECHNOLOGY USED
1 1982 US 4,341,502 Planar Closed-Chain Positioning Hiroshi Makino (Yamanashi / Sankyo) Articulated arms deflect vertically during part insertion. Original SCARA configuration: XY compliance with Z rigidity. 2-link revolute horizontal chain ($L_1, L_2$) + quill.
2 2015 US 9,035,210 B2 Optical Robotic Sorting Apparatus Charles R. Bratney (Bratney Co.) Color shifts and random item angles jam mechanical gates. Optical signature analysis with dynamic robotic pick-and-place. RGB line-scan cameras, pixel segmentation, pneumatic grasp.
3 2020 US 10,836,033 B2 Robot (Integrated Drive Control) Kazuo Kawasumi (Seiko Epson) External cable harnesses cause joint wear and electrical noise. Modular motor driver boards embedded inside arm link cavities. Embedded driver PCBs, hollow-joint routing, internal digital bus.
4 2020 US 10,710,119 B2 Material Sorting Using Vision Nalin Kumar et al. (UHV Tech) Ambient light variation corrupts color threshold accuracy. Illumination-invariant multi-channel color feature extraction. RGB & HSV color spaces, contour bounding, conveyor sync.
5 2020 US 10,793,369 B2 Conveyor Tracking & Handling Timothy Shaffer (Omron Adept) Stopping conveyors for picking drops throughput drastically. Vision + encoder synchronization for on-the-fly robotic picking. Hand-eye homography calibration, moving frame transformations.
6 2023 US 2023/0295847 A1 Item Vision Robotic Sorting Station Bradley Porter et al. (Amazon) Specular glare and overlapping items cause pick errors. Multi-camera illumination workstation with bounding regression. RGB-D vision, CNN feature extraction, robotic divert routing.
7 2018 US 9,981,387 B2 Synchronous Serial Bus Joint Control Takashi Harada (Yaskawa / FANUC) Point-to-point wiring fatigue & clock skew across multi-axis arms. Daisy-chain differential serial bus linking smart joint nodes. 4-wire differential CAN / serial bus, 14-bit magnetic encoders.
PATENT 01 (1982) US 4,341,502
Planar Closed-Chain Positioning
Hiroshi Makino (Yamanashi Univ. / Sankyo)

Pioneered SCARA configuration: High planar XY compliance for assembly insertion with vertical Z-axis rigidity.

PATENT 02 (2015) US 9,035,210 B2
Optical Robotic Sorting Apparatus
Charles R. Bratney (Bratney Co.)

Combines optical chromatic signature analysis with multi-arm robotic pick-and-place end-effectors for real-time sorting.

PATENT 03 (2020) US 10,836,033 B2
Robot (Integrated Drive Control)
Kazuo Kawasumi (Seiko Epson)

Embeds motor driver boards directly inside arm link cavities to eliminate bulky external controller cabinets.

PATENT 04 (2020) US 10,710,119 B2
Material Sorting Using Vision
Nalin Kumar et al. (UHV Tech)

Illumination-invariant multi-channel color space feature extraction for sorting objects despite ambient lighting shifts.

PATENT 05 (2020) US 10,793,369 B2
Conveyor Tracking & Handling
Timothy Shaffer (Omron Adept)

Hand-eye homography calibration matrix and encoder tracking to pick parts from moving conveyors without stopping.

PATENT 07 (2018) US 9,981,387 B2
Synchronous Serial Bus Joint Control
Takashi Harada (Yaskawa / FANUC)

4-wire differential daisy-chained serial bus (CAN) connecting smart motor drivers with magnetic encoder feedback.
